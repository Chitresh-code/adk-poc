# Expansion play: advanced routing upsell at a logistics account

A logistics customer's monthly active users grew steadily for two
consecutive quarters as their ops team expanded rollout past the original
pilot group. During the growth, their ops lead started asking about
capabilities in the advanced routing and reporting tier that weren't
included in their current plan. CS brought that request to the account's
next QBR with concrete usage data showing which teams had already adopted
the base product, framing the upsell as matching their existing rollout
rather than a new ask. The account upgraded to the advanced tier the same
quarter, with the AE closing the commercial terms.
