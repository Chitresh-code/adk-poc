# Playbook: recognizing cross-sell signals

Sustained usage growth (15% or more quarter over quarter) combined with no
open critical or high-severity tickets and neutral-to-positive sentiment is
the standard signal to raise a cross-sell conversation, not just log it as a
healthy account. Look for feature requests that map to a paid add-on tier
before proposing anything: an upsell grounded in something the account
already asked for closes faster than one pitched cold. Loop in the
account's AE before the conversation happens, not after, so pricing and
terms are ready if the account says yes on the call.
