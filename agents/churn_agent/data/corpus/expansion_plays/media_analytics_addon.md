# Expansion play: analytics add-on at a media account

A media customer maintained flat, steady usage for several quarters with no
complaints and no growth. Rather than treating flat usage as neutral, CS
reviewed their feature-adoption gap and found they'd never turned on the
built-in content-performance analytics module, despite it being included at
their tier. A short enablement call, not a sales pitch, got the module
adopted, which increased their weekly active usage by roughly a third the
following quarter and became the basis for a later upsell conversation.
