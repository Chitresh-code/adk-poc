# Retention play: sponsor turnover at a financial services account

A financial services customer's executive sponsor moved to a different
internal team mid-contract, and the account went quiet for six weeks with
no CS engagement from the new stakeholder. Rather than waiting for the
renewal conversation to surface the gap, the CSM proactively scheduled a
re-onboarding call with the incoming sponsor, walked through the account's
usage history and prior wins, and re-established a named point of contact
on both sides. Framing it as a re-onboarding rather than a save conversation
kept the tone collaborative instead of defensive. The account renewed on
schedule with no discount required.
