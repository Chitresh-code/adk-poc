# Playbook: critical and high-severity ticket escalation

Any open critical or high-severity ticket older than 48 hours on an account
already showing a usage or sentiment decline should be treated as a churn
signal on its own, not just a support metric. CS should get a status update
directly from the engineering owner of the ticket, not rely on the support
queue's SLA timer, and proactively communicate progress to the account
before they ask. A resolved escalation handled this way, with a follow-up
from CS rather than only from support, consistently shows up as a positive
mention in the account's next QBR.
