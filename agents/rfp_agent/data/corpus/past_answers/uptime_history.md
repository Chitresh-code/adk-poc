# Past answer: historical uptime

**Question asked (Q4 2025 RFP, retail prospect):** What was your actual
uptime over the last 12 months, and where can we verify it?

**Answer given:** Measured uptime for the trailing 12 months was 99.96%
against our 99.9% SLA commitment, with one incident (a 47-minute partial
outage in a single region) counted against it. Historical status and
incident postmortems are published on our public status page; we retain
12 months of history there. We do not provide raw monitoring data or
third-party-audited uptime figures beyond what's on the status page.
