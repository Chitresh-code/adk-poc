# Past answer: SOC 2 certification

**Question asked (Q3 2025 RFP, mid-market insurance prospect):** Are you SOC 2
certified, and can you share the report?

**Answer given:** Northbound holds a SOC 2 Type II report covering the
Security and Availability trust principles, audited annually by a
third-party firm. The current report covers the twelve-month period ending
March 2025. We share the full report under NDA during active procurement;
a public-facing summary is available on request without NDA. We do not
currently carry a Type II report for the Confidentiality or Privacy trust
principles, though our controls address both informally.
