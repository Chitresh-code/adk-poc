# Past answer: pricing model and discounts

**Question asked (Q2 2025 RFP, mid-market prospect, 200 seats):** How is
pricing structured, and what discounts apply at our seat count?

**Answer given:** Pricing is per named seat per month, billed annually,
with three tiers (Starter, Business, Enterprise) differentiated by SSO/
SCIM, API rate limits, and support SLA. At 200 seats a customer qualifies
for our volume discount band (150+ seats), typically 15-20% off list,
finalized by sales based on term length and plan tier. We do not publish
exact discount percentages; they're negotiated per deal. Multi-year
commitments unlock an additional discount, typically 5-10% per additional
year up to a 3-year term.
