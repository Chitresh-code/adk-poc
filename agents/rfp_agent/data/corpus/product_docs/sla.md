# Product doc: uptime SLA and support tiers

Committed uptime SLA is 99.9% monthly for Business and Enterprise plans,
measured on core application availability (excludes scheduled maintenance
windows, announced at least 5 business days in advance). Starter plan has
no financial SLA, best-effort only.

Service credits for SLA breaches: 10% of monthly fees credited for uptime
between 99.0% and 99.9%, 25% for uptime below 99.0%, capped at one month
of fees per incident window. Credits must be requested within 30 days of
the breach; they are not applied automatically.

Support response targets (Enterprise): P1 (production down) 1 hour, P2
(major feature impaired) 4 business hours, P3 (minor issue) 1 business
day. Business plan targets are roughly double; Starter is best-effort
email support with no committed response time.
