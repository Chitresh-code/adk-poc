# Product doc: compliance certifications

Current certifications and attestations:

- SOC 2 Type II (Security, Availability), renewed annually.
- ISO 27001 certified since 2024, surveillance audits annually.
- GDPR: we act as data processor for EU customer data; a signed DPA is
  available through legal during contracting, standard contractual clauses
  included for any sub-processor transfers outside the EU.
- HIPAA: we are not currently a HIPAA-eligible vendor and do not sign
  Business Associate Agreements. Customers in healthcare should not submit
  PHI to Northbound.
- PCI DSS: not applicable, we do not process or store payment card data;
  billing is handled entirely by our payments sub-processor.
