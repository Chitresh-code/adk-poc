# Security and Vendor Questionnaire: Northbound Platform

Prepared for vendor response as part of a mid-market procurement process.
Please answer each question directly; where a policy document exists,
referencing it is acceptable.

## Section 1: Security and Compliance

1. Are you SOC 2 certified? If so, which trust principles are covered and
   can you provide the report?
2. Are you ISO 27001 certified?
3. Do you sign a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA)?
4. Describe your vulnerability management program, including penetration
   testing frequency.
5. What is your process for notifying customers of a data breach, and
   within what timeframe?

## Section 2: Data Handling

6. Where is customer data physically stored? Can data residency be
   restricted to a specific region?
7. What is your data retention and deletion policy after contract
   termination?
8. Describe your encryption practices for data at rest and in transit.
9. Do you support customer-managed encryption keys (BYOK/CMEK)?

## Section 3: Identity and Access

10. Do you support SAML SSO? Which identity providers have been tested?
11. Is SCIM provisioning supported?
12. Can multi-factor authentication be enforced organization-wide?
13. What role-based access controls are available, and is audit logging
    included?

## Section 4: Availability and Support

14. What uptime SLA do you commit to, and what has actual uptime been over
    the last 12 months?
15. What are your backup frequency and disaster recovery objectives (RPO/
    RTO)?
16. What support response times apply at our plan tier?

## Section 5: Commercial

17. How is pricing structured, and what discounts are available at volume?
18. Are multi-year commitments discounted?

## Section 6: Other

19. Do you offer an on-premise or self-hosted deployment option for
    customers who cannot use a multi-tenant cloud service?
20. What is your policy on carbon offsets or sustainability reporting for
    your cloud infrastructure usage?
