# Case study: Ashgrove Capital audit readiness

Ashgrove Capital, an enterprise financial services firm, adopted Northbound
ahead of an internal audit cycle that required demonstrable data access
controls and a clean change-management trail. Their CTO's office cited
Northbound's built-in audit logging and role-based access controls as the
deciding factor over a competitor that would have required a custom logging
layer to meet the same bar. The audit closed without any findings related to
the platform layer, the first time that had happened in three audit cycles.
