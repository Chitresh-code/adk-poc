# Proof point: switching from a legacy platform

Across the deals that closed in the past year, the most common trigger
wasn't a feature gap, it was operational cost: teams running a legacy
platform were spending more engineer-hours on babysitting infrastructure
than on the product work that infrastructure was supposed to support.
Customers who switched to Northbound consistently cited a reduction in
on-call load and a faster path to provisioning new environments as the two
changes they noticed first, usually within the first month.
