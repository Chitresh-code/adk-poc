# Case study: Cartwell Freight scaling event

Cartwell Freight, an enterprise logistics customer, used Northbound to
absorb a 4x traffic spike during a peak shipping season after a competitor's
platform had buckled the year before under similar load. Their VP of
Engineering had specifically evaluated Northbound's autoscaling behavior
under sustained write load before signing, running a two-week load test
against a staging environment. No downtime was reported during the actual
peak window, and their infrastructure spend for that quarter came in lower
than the prior year despite the higher traffic.
