# Case study: Riverbend Health platform migration

Riverbend Health, a mid-market healthcare provider network, migrated its
internal reporting stack onto Northbound over a six-week window in Q1 2025.
Their platform engineering team had outgrown a self-managed Postgres cluster
and wanted managed infrastructure without giving up direct SQL access.
Northbound's HIPAA-eligible deployment tier and existing SOC 2 report let
their compliance review close in under two weeks, faster than the four to
six weeks they'd budgeted. Post-migration, their on-call platform team
reported a meaningful drop in weekend paging for storage-related incidents.
